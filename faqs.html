Services & projects

What does INJIVA Digital Systems do?

We focus on software engineering, data engineering and business intelligence, AI and automation, and cloud and managed support. The scope of each engagement is agreed around the client’s needs.

Can you improve an existing system?

Start by describing the system and the problem you want to solve. Access, documentation, technical constraints and ownership need to be assessed before any work is agreed. Do not send passwords in an initial enquiry.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, integrations, delivery requirements and support. An initial enquiry is not a quotation. Fees, deliverables and payment terms must be agreed in writing.

How long does delivery take?

A reliable schedule depends on the agreed requirements, dependencies and available capacity. Milestones and responsibilities are confirmed during scoping; this website does not promise a fixed delivery time.

Can we work together remotely or across borders?

Contact us to discuss your location, time zone and requirements. Contracting, data access, support arrangements and any cross-border obligations need to be agreed for the particular engagement.

Who owns the delivered work?

Ownership, licences, third-party components and handover materials are covered by the signed project agreement. These terms should be agreed before development begins.

Is ongoing support included?

Support is scoped separately or explicitly included in your agreement. Confirm coverage, channels, response targets, maintenance and renewal terms before relying on a support arrangement.

Internships

Are internship applications currently open?

This site does not list a confirmed open intake. You can enquire about availability through the internships page. An enquiry does not reserve a place or confirm selection. Internship enquiries go to internships@injivadigitalsystems.com.

Which internship areas can I ask about?

Areas include software engineering, data engineering, data science and analytics, AI engineering, database administration, and software testing and quality assurance.

Are internships paid, and how long do they last?

Pay or allowances, duration, hours and working arrangements depend on the specific placement and must be confirmed in writing. This page makes no promise of payment or a particular duration.

Can students, graduates or self-taught candidates enquire?

You may enquire and explain your learning background. Eligibility and selection criteria are specific to an available opportunity; an enquiry is not a guarantee of acceptance.

Should I upload my CV or identity documents?

There is no upload facility. Start with a brief introduction and, optionally, a public portfolio link. Wait for instructions before sharing a CV, and do not send identity documents or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

Will an internship guarantee a job or certificate?

No such guarantee is made on this website. Any assessment, completion documentation, academic credit or future employment arrangements must be confirmed for the individual placement.

Website & privacy

Does the enquiry form send my message automatically?

No. It prepares an email draft in your browser. Review the draft, open your configured email app and send it yourself. If your email app does not open, copy the text into a new email to info@injivadigitalsystems.com.

Does this website use advertising trackers or analytics?

The supplied website code includes no advertising trackers, analytics scripts or application cookies. Hosting providers can process technical request information. Read the cookie and privacy notices for the scope of these statements.

Do the technology logos indicate partnerships?

No. They illustrate technology options and link to the relevant product websites. They do not claim INJIVA certifications, partnership status, endorsement or an established implementation capability.

How do I ask about my personal information?

Email info@injivadigitalsystems.com with the subject “Privacy request”. Describe your request without including unnecessary identity documents. The available rights and exceptions depend on applicable law.

How do I report an accessibility problem?

Email info@injivadigitalsystems.com or call +263 77 105 0052. Include the page and the difficulty you experienced, and the format or assistance you need. Read the accessibility statement for the current assessment limits.
