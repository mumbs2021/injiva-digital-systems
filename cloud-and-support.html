Our delivery approach

What we establish together

System inventory, hosting access, existing contracts, operational priorities and an agreed support scope.

What the engagement can produce

How we keep it accountable

Agree scope and acceptance criteria, review progress, test the solution and document responsibilities before handover.

USEFUL TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Do you provide 24/7 support?

Coverage and response arrangements must be agreed in writing. This website does not promise continuous support or a universal response time.

Can you manage existing hosting?

A review establishes access, architecture, costs and operational risks before a management arrangement is agreed.

Are hosting fees included?

Hosting, provider subscriptions and ongoing support are identified separately in the commercial scope.

What happens at handover?

The handover should include relevant access, deployment instructions, operating guidance and an agreed route for support.
