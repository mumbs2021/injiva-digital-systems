USEFUL TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Does every automation need AI?

No. Rules and standard integrations are often more predictable for structured tasks. AI should be used where it adds justified value.

How is accuracy assessed?

We agree representative examples and evaluation criteria. Limitations and failure cases should be documented alongside successful results.

Will confidential data train external models?

Provider settings, agreements and permitted data use must be assessed before any data is sent. We do not assume that all services have the same terms.

What happens when the system is uncertain?

The workflow should define escalation, human review or a safe fallback. High-impact actions require controls appropriate to their consequences.
