USEFUL TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Can you work with spreadsheet data?

Yes. A review establishes its structure and quality, then identifies whether a managed spreadsheet workflow or a database-backed approach is appropriate.

Will reporting update automatically?

Refresh frequency depends on source availability, integration access and hosting. We define and test that behaviour as part of the scope.

How do you handle sensitive data?

Access, environments, retention and permitted uses are agreed before data is shared. Development should use representative, de-identified data where practical.

Can a dashboard guarantee better results?

A dashboard supports decisions. Its value depends on reliable definitions, suitable data and how the organisation acts on the information.
