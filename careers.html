WHAT GOOD WORK LOOKS LIKE

Ability matters.
How you work matters too.

These principles guide a useful career conversation. Specific responsibilities and performance expectations belong in the confirmed role description.

01

Make your thinking clear.

Explain decisions, document work and communicate issues early. Show what you contributed and what you learned.

02

Respect the work and the people.

Handle information carefully, honour agreed responsibilities and collaborate with colleagues across disciplines.

03

Improve through feedback.

Ask questions, test assumptions and use review to strengthen the quality of your contribution.

A CLEAR ROUTE FORWARD

From curiosity to a considered next step.

  1. 01

    Choose your area

    Identify the department that best matches your interests and experience.

    Confirm the opportunity
