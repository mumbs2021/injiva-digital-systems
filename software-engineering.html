USEFUL TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Can you improve an existing system?

An initial review establishes its condition, constraints and ownership. We then agree whether a focused improvement, integration or replacement is appropriate.

Who owns the source code?

Source-code rights, third-party licences and handover materials are agreed in the project contract before implementation.

How are changes handled?

Changes are assessed against the agreed scope. Their impact on effort, cost and timing is discussed before additional work begins.

Is maintenance included?

The project agreement defines warranty, maintenance and support arrangements. Ongoing support can be scoped separately.
