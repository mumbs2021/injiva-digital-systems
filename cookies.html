DEVICE PRIVACY

Cookies & tracking

A clear account of what this website’s code does on your device.

Cookies and browser storage

The supplied site code sets no cookies and does not use localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or a service worker. It includes no analytics, advertising pixels, behavioural tracking, third-party fonts or embedded videos. It does not sell or share data through advertising technology.

Why there is no cookie banner

No optional tracking technology is implemented here, so the website does not present a consent banner for tracking it does not perform. Hosting or security services can behave independently of this code and must be assessed for the deployed website.

Temporary form information

Form text remains in the page while you use it. Your browser may retain autofill information or restore page state. Copying a draft uses the clipboard only when requested. These features are described in the privacy notice.

External links

Technology logos link to external product websites. They are locally hosted images and do not contact those companies when displayed. Following an external link takes you to a site with its own cookie and privacy practices.

If tracking is added later

Any change to analytics, advertising, embedded services or device storage requires a fresh review and an updated notice. Where prior consent is required, optional technology must remain blocked until the user chooses it, with a usable refusal and withdrawal mechanism.
