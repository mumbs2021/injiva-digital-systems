ACCESS FOR EVERY VISITOR

Accessibility statement

We aim to make our content and contact options usable by people with different access needs.

Design target and assessment status

The design target is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is a target, not a certification or claim of full conformance. The site has not undergone a comprehensive independent accessibility audit or testing with all assistive technologies.

Features included

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, descriptive links and a skip-to-content link.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation disclosures and FAQ accordions.
  • Visible focus indicators, labelled form controls and native input validation.
  • Layouts that adapt to narrow screens; text remains available without JavaScript.
  • Reduced-motion support and no automatically playing media.
  • Visible explanations of enquiry drafting, with direct email and telephone alternatives.

Known limitations

Email drafting depends on your device’s configured mail application. Clipboard support varies, with manual copying available as an alternative. Third-party websites linked from this site are outside our control. Browser and assistive-technology combinations, zoom and mobile interaction need continued review.

Report a barrier or request another format

Email info@injivadigitalsystems.com or call +263 77 105 0052. Tell us which page or task caused difficulty and the help or format you need. Device and browser details are useful but optional; you do not need to disclose a diagnosis.

Review

Last reviewed: 30 October 2026. Accessibility feedback should inform future fixes and testing. This statement does not remove any rights available under applicable accessibility or equality law.
