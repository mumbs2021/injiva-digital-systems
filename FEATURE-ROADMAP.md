# Suggested next features

1. Real project case studies: problem, solution, approved screenshots and measured results. Use a browser-local industry filter once there are enough projects.
2. Interactive service finder: three short questions leading to the appropriate service page and a prefilled enquiry. Keep all recommendations explainable.
3. Visual delivery timeline: expand Discover, Define, Build and Launch to show deliverables and client involvement.
4. Team profiles: approved portraits, roles and authentic professional profile links.
5. Insights: substantive articles on data quality, automation and software operations with author and review dates.
6. A downloadable capability statement: concise PDF covering services, approach and verified contact details.
7. Booking: add a link only after the company supplies its actual scheduling URL.
8. Language options: use professionally reviewed translations and distinct URLs rather than a nonfunctional language selector.

## Technology marks
The homepage now includes Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, GitHub, TensorFlow and PyTorch marks, with descriptive links to their product websites. They are presented as illustrative technology options; no client, partner, certification or existing capability claim is made. Confirm actual technology offerings and appropriate brand-use permissions before public launch. Do not label these logos “Trusted by” or “Our partners” without evidence.

SVG assets: Devicon v2.16.0, https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/v2.16.0/icons . Original filenames use <name>-original.svg except amazonwebservices-original-wordmark.svg. The upstream license is included in assets/images/technology/DEVICON-LICENSE.txt; it does not itself grant trademark permission.
Brand guidance: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks and https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/ .

No runtime libraries, external fonts or CDN calls were added. Logos are local SVG files. Native links work without JavaScript. The site remains a static HTML/CSS/JS package.
