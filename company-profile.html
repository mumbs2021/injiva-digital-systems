Who we are

INJIVA Digital Systems is a business unit of INJIVA, operated by Ascendia Learning Enterprises (Private) Limited t/a INJIVA in Zimbabwe. The business focuses on software, data, AI and practical digital systems.

One organisation, connected capabilities

INJIVA brings together INJIVA Academy and INJIVA Digital Systems. INJIVA Academy focuses on learning and skills development; INJIVA Digital Systems focuses on applying technical expertise to organisational challenges.

Leadership

Ruvimbo Mumbire is the Founder & Chief Executive Officer of INJIVA, encompassing INJIVA Academy and INJIVA Digital Systems.
